项目Projects

ZeroOpera

2026.05 – 至今present

基于大模型先验修复的零样本戏曲歌声合成

Zero-shot Peking opera singing voice synthesis via prior-guided inpainting

  • 声学修复(Acoustic Inpainting):在低资源垂域上直接微调 SVS 基座模型,容易出现灾难性遗忘并削弱音色克隆能力。我们用流行乐基座生成的 Mel 谱作为结构先验,通过掩码机制局部重构、全局增强戏曲特有技巧,保留基座的 zero-shot 音色泛化能力,同时完成跨域风格迁移。
  • 技巧驱动的音系调制(TPM):针对尖团音、上口字等戏曲特有发音带来的 OOV 问题,将离散的发音规则映射为连续控制信号,在语言特征层做 FiLM 调制,不再依赖细粒度音素对齐,实现对未见音节的零样本泛化。
  • 结果:相比强基座 SoulX-Singer,发音错误率(PER)降低 19.4%,音色相似度提升 0.43 MOS。第一作者,论文在投 ICLR 2027。
  • Acoustic inpainting. Fine-tuning an SVS foundation model on a small, specialized domain tends to cause catastrophic forgetting and degrade timbre cloning. We use a Mel-spectrogram generated by a pop-music base model as a structural prior, then reconstruct it locally with a mask and enhance opera-specific techniques globally — keeping the base model's zero-shot timbre generalization while transferring style across domains.
  • Technique-driven Phonological Modulation (TPM). Opera-specific pronunciations (e.g. jian-tuan yin, shang-kou zi) create out-of-vocabulary problems. TPM maps discrete pronunciation rules to continuous control signals and applies FiLM modulation at the linguistic-feature level, removing the need for fine-grained phoneme alignment and generalizing zero-shot to unseen syllables.
  • Results. Versus the strong SoulX-Singer baseline: 19.4% lower phoneme error rate (PER) and +0.43 MOS in timbre similarity. First-author paper, under review at ICLR 2027.

字级别歌唱技巧控制Word-level singing-technique control

2026.05 – 至今present

零样本字级别歌唱技巧控制歌声合成

Zero-shot singing voice synthesis with controllable singing techniques

  • 大规模数据挖掘与清洗:独立完成跨平台超 200 万首原始音频采集,设计自动化清洗策略,提纯出 55 万首高质量干声数据。
  • 特征解耦:基于开源 SoulX-Singer 做二次开发。针对 zero-shot 音色克隆中音色与演唱技巧高度耦合的问题,引入 GeMAPS 声学参数集进行解耦。
  • 细粒度可控生成:提取段落级声学特征并无监督聚类,挖掘出与气声、烟嗓、高爆发等技巧相关的潜在表征;由声学规划模块预测并作为 condition 引入生成网络,实现对演唱技巧的独立控制。字级别控制正在开发中。
  • Large-scale data mining. Collected 2M+ raw tracks across platforms and built an automatic cleaning pipeline that yields 550K high-quality dry-vocal recordings.
  • Feature disentanglement. Built on the open-source SoulX-Singer. To address the entanglement of timbre and singing technique in zero-shot cloning, we bring in the GeMAPS acoustic feature set.
  • Fine-grained control. Segment-level acoustic features are clustered without supervision to surface latent representations tied to techniques such as breathy, smoky, and belting voice. An acoustic planner predicts them and feeds them to the generator as conditions, giving independent control over technique. Word-level control is in development.

生成式先验的高维时空建模Generative priors for spatiotemporal modeling

2025.03 – 2026.04

高维时空序列建模与零样本生成

High-dimensional spatiotemporal modeling and zero-shot generation

  • 核心算法:面向 CFD 超高分辨率风场、CMIP6 多模式模拟场与真实观测不匹配的问题,主导设计基于 Flow Matching 与潜空间扩散的生成框架,缓解传统物理模型计算慢、精度低,以及传统降尺度依赖成对数据的限制。
  • 零样本后验采样:提出基于潜空间 Diffusion Posterior Sampling 的零样本统计降尺度算法,结合扩散先验与渐进式蒸馏,无需重新训练即可高保真重建。
  • 多源融合与域迁移:用多任务学习和注意力机制对齐、融合不同地学模态,提升跨域鲁棒性。
  • 产出:Nature Communications 论文(共同一作)、NeurIPS 2026 论文(一作)、EGU 2026 highlight oral。
  • Core algorithms. Real observations rarely match ultra-high-resolution CFD wind fields or multi-model CMIP6 simulations. I led the design of generative frameworks based on flow matching and latent diffusion, addressing the slowness and limited accuracy of physical models and the reliance of classical downscaling on paired data.
  • Zero-shot posterior sampling. Proposed a zero-shot statistical downscaling method built on latent diffusion posterior sampling, combining a diffusion prior with progressive distillation for high-fidelity reconstruction without retraining.
  • Multi-source fusion & domain transfer. Multi-task learning and attention to align and fuse heterogeneous geoscience modalities, improving cross-domain robustness.
  • Outputs. A Nature Communications paper (co-first author), a NeurIPS 2026 paper (first author), and an EGU 2026 highlight oral.
