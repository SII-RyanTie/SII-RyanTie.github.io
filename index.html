我是复旦大学与上海创智学院联合培养的博士生。本科毕业于西安电子科技大学,硕士阶段在战略支援部队航天工程大学与国家气象信息中心联合培养,从深度学习气象降尺度做起;随后在部队担任作战指挥参谋两年,再回到国家气象信息中心和上海科学智能研究院做科研,2025 年秋季开始读博。

我关心的是:预训练好的生成式先验,能不能在不重新训练的前提下被复用到新的任务和新的域上。目前这条线分成两个方向:一是零样本歌声合成,特别是戏曲这类低资源垂域,以及演唱技巧的可控生成;二是用 Flow Matching 和扩散模型做气候与高维时空数据的降尺度。

I am a Ph.D. student at Fudan University and Shanghai Innovation Institute. I did my B.S. at Xidian University and my M.S. at the PLA Space Engineering University jointly with the National Meteorological Information Center (NMIC), starting out with deep-learning downscaling of weather fields. After two years as an operations staff officer in the PLA, I went back to research at NMIC and the Shanghai Academy of AI for Science, and began my Ph.D. in the fall of 2025.

The question I keep coming back to: can a pretrained generative prior be reused on a new task or domain without retraining? Right now that plays out in two places: zero-shot singing voice synthesis, especially low-resource domains like Peking opera and controllable singing techniques; and downscaling climate and other high-dimensional spatiotemporal data with flow matching and diffusion models.

ICLR2027
ZeroOpera: Zero-Shot Peking Opera Singing Voice Synthesis via Prior-Guided Inpainting and Enhancement
R. Tie, et al.
在投 ICLR 2027,第一作者Under review at ICLR 2027 · first author

在低资源的戏曲数据上直接微调歌声合成基座会遗忘音色克隆能力。我们把流行乐基座生成的 Mel 谱当作结构先验,用掩码做局部修复与全局增强;再用技巧驱动的音系调制模块(TPM)对尖团音、上口字等未见发音做 FiLM 调制,不依赖细粒度音素对齐。相比 SoulX-Singer,PER 降低 19.4%,音色相似度提升 0.43 MOS。

Fine-tuning a singing-voice foundation model on small opera data makes it forget zero-shot timbre cloning. We treat a Mel-spectrogram from a pop-music base model as a structural prior, then inpaint locally with a mask and enhance opera techniques globally. A technique-driven phonological modulation module (TPM) applies FiLM modulation for unseen opera pronunciations, with no fine-grained phoneme alignment. Versus SoulX-Singer: 19.4% lower PER and +0.43 MOS timbre similarity.

SVS2026
零样本、字级别的歌唱技巧控制Zero-shot, word-level control of singing techniques

在开源的 SoulX-Singer 上做二次开发。针对音色与演唱技巧耦合的问题,引入 GeMAPS 声学特征做解耦;对段落级声学特征做无监督聚类,挖出与气声、烟嗓、高爆发等技巧对应的潜在表征,由声学规划模块预测后作为条件送入生成网络。为此我自己采集了超过 200 万首原始音频,清洗得到 55 万首高质量干声。字级别控制正在开发中。

Built on the open-source SoulX-Singer. To untangle timbre from singing technique, we bring in GeMAPS acoustic features and cluster segment-level features without supervision, surfacing latent representations for breathy, smoky and belting voice; an acoustic planner predicts them as conditions for the generator. I collected 2M+ raw tracks and cleaned them down to 550K high-quality dry vocals. Word-level control is in development.
