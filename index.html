I am a Ph.D. student at Fudan University and Shanghai Innovation Institute. I started out in AI for atmospheric science, where my work centered on downscaling meteorological and climate data with deep learning and, later, generative models. I now work on generative audio: singing voice synthesis, including low-resource styles such as Peking opera, and streaming music generation.
